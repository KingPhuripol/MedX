# Progress Readiness Workflow

Target: `$ARGUMENTS`.

Official Progress Report deadline is 4 Dec 2026. Evidence/rough target 20 Nov; advisor-ready 29 Nov; freeze 1 Dec; readiness 3 Dec. Presentation is 14-15 Dec, planned against 14 Dec; advisor-ready 9 Dec; freeze 11 Dec; final rehearsal 13 Dec.

1. Read schedule, charter/decisions, milestones/tasks/risks/status, all relevant specs/contracts, manifests/evaluation records, code/test evidence, reviewer verdicts, and target requirements.
2. Create an evidence inventory with revision/version/checksum for data/split audits, fixed baseline, dynamic DAG, graph export/replay/adaptation, preliminary controlled results, mock/gateway/product alpha, safety/audit/human-review, and Research adapter status.
3. Compare planned vs actual. Explain deviations, failed runs, carry-over, and scope decisions; never rewrite a missing result as progress.
4. Verify every reported number/table/figure traces to valid manifests/evaluations and includes denominator, split/snapshot, model/config, compute, uncertainty, and limitations.
5. Require leakage/license/authorization status and invalidate affected results on confirmed failure.
6. Check safety-first Clinical Front Door evidence: urgent/red flag, under-triage/false reassurance plan or result, abstention, invalid provider, human confirmation, audit, synthetic/default external payload.
7. Check integration through shared fixtures and versions, not separate component success.
8. Update milestone confidence, risks, compute/data feasibility, critical path, workload, and next-semester gated plan. Keep 27B out unless separately approved.
9. For presentation, require evidence-first story, failure/limitation transparency, functioning primary/offline demo, timed rehearsal, readable figures, and Q&A.
10. Obtain independent safety/integration reviews. Verdict `NOT_READY`, `CONDITIONALLY_READY`, or `ADVISOR_READY`. Do not submit or publish.

Return deadline/buffer, evidence completeness matrix, invalid/missing evidence, planned-vs-actual, technical/product/safety/integration verdicts, risks/decisions, next-semester critical path, report/deck/demo verdict, and owner/date actions.
